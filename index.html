ANA Business Class · New York → Tokyo

Partner award seats bookable with Virgin Atlantic points. Checked automatically every 4 hours.

Found a date? How to book

  1. Double-check it's still there by searching that date on united.com (with "Book with miles") or ana.co.jp. If an ANA nonstop shows in business at the lowest ("saver") price, Virgin should see it too.
  2. Call Virgin Atlantic Flying Club (the US number is on virginatlantic.com's contact page). Give the date and the ANA nonstop from JFK, and ask them to confirm they see the seat and what the points and taxes come to.
  3. Transfer Amex Membership Rewards → Virgin Atlantic (1:1) only once the agent confirms. Transfers usually land within minutes, but they can't be undone.
  4. Ticket it on the same call if possible. Seats can disappear within hours.
The rate last reported for NYC→Japan in ANA business is about 60,000 Virgin points one-way, plus taxes and fees. Confirm the price with the agent before transferring.
